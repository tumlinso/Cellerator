# Discovery and certification benchmark v1

Migrated support-sketch, signature, atom-plane, fragment, grammar-basis, and
exact-scan mechanisms report proposal count, exact rescans, certifications,
rejections, wall time, and peak bytes against size/support-matched generic and
null baselines. No-basis inputs are mandatory. Correct negative outcomes are
retained as `evaluated_not_promoted`; no candidate is promoted without exact
certification. Runs use eleven samples under the benchmark mutex.
