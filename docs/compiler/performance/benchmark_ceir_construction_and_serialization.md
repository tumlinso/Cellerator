# CEIR construction and serialization benchmark v1

Semantic, Planning, Realization, and Executable IR are crossed with construction,
canonicalization, text parse/print, binary load/store, memory mapping, unknown
extension preservation, and provenance stripping. Each cell reports nodes/s,
bytes/node, peak RSS, artifact bytes, and eleven raw timings under the benchmark
mutex. Round trips require structural equality and retained unknown extensions.
