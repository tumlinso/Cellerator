the reviewer then sent an independently formed verdict and read the root record.
Both agree on the bounded 6.13% resident-time reduction and the absence of a
production/generalization claim. The reviewer explicitly distinguished the
ratio of medians from the median individual paired ratio and emphasized that
within-run blocks do not characterize between-run variation.

inspection shows that a global stable selection can be triggered by tied-bank

rebuilt together in Release, so an old/debug native archive is not an explanation.

The reviewer agreed this is a plausible source-derived lead, but challenged
prioritizing it from this evidence: the private arms also change ownership and
use 33.33x as much tape/scratch. One motif cannot establish the hardware cause,
rank that lead against memory cost, or justify a general routing change.

Resolution: retain that question beside the measured factoring result. No next
experiment, implementation priority, profiling job, or production route change
was selected. Return the result and tradeoffs to the user for discussion.
