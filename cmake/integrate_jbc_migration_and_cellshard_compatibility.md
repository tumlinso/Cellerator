# JBC migration and CellShard compatibility receipt

The embedded CellShard revision remains
`b9749ad3e5146a04f847533d8c6f1a54146aed20`, the source revision cited by the
JBC provenance and ownership records. No later gitlink is required by the
validated compatibility boundary, so this integration does not manufacture a
submodule advance.

Cellerator owns discovery, evidence, atom/basis/composition semantics, exact
certification, portable schedules, and compiled ruleset export. CellShard owns
concrete storage, persistence, placement, transport, materialization, and
runtime binding. The combined-build bridge consumes the opaque Cellerator
artifact without returning compiler policy to CellShard. Historical JBC source
and tests remain intact as provenance and compatibility evidence.
