# E37 — Competing parse forests

Owner: `BP-MOON-110`. Suggested implementation location: `experiments/moonshot/families/hypotheses/e37`.

## Question
Can the encoder postpone a bad boundary decision instead of irreversibly collapsing alternatives?

## Concrete mechanism
Keep a small beam of alternative segmentations or local grammar states, sharing common prefixes and exact source references. Higher-level queries prune or refine alternatives; use a bounded packed forest rather than copying the entire sequence representation.

## Mapping onto Volta
Small state masks, lane-per-hypothesis tables, compact backpointers and uniform beam updates.

## What this does not establish
A beam is approximate and can discard the correct interpretation. Its scores are not automatically posterior probabilities.

## First small executable probe
A deliberately ambiguous boundary has two valid interpretations; a later query resolves them differently.

Write a scalar or algebraic fixture and the smallest representative machine path. Prefer one intelligible executable mechanism over an empty API shell. Leave alternative variants visible.
