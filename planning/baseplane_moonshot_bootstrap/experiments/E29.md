# E29 — Continuous gates hardened to LOP3

Numerical owner: `CE-MOON-040` in Cellerator. Sequence consumer: `BP-MOON-090` in Baseplane. Numerical implementation location: `experiments/baseplane_moonshot/families/learning/e29`.

## Question
Can useful local learning eventually become instruction-level structure?

## Concrete mechanism
Learn a small Boolean network through relaxed gates or a small truth-table model, discretize it and emit native constant-LUT kernels. Keep the floating teacher and hard circuit separately versioned, and compare their decisions before reusing the circuit as a route.

## Mapping onto Volta
LOP3 immediates plus masks; optional offline code generation, not runtime NVRTC as a prerequisite.

## What this does not establish
A learned circuit is not automatically scientifically interpretable. Truth-table fit alone says nothing about generalization to biological data.

## First small executable probe
Fit a synthetic gate, emit its eight-bit LUT and exhaustively check all rows; log hardening disagreement on a larger fixture later.

Write a scalar or algebraic fixture and the smallest representative machine path. Prefer one intelligible executable mechanism over an empty API shell. Leave alternative variants visible. Record a failed compile or unresolved semantic issue instead of hiding it or spending the whole campaign on qualification.
