# E44 — Counterfactual packet worlds

Numerical owner: `CE-MOON-050` in Cellerator. Sequence consumer: `BP-MOON-120` in Baseplane. Numerical implementation location: `experiments/baseplane_moonshot/families/mechanisms/e44`.

## Question
Can alternative sequences or cellular conditions share almost all their execution?

## Concrete mechanism
Carry a world ID and a sparse delta over an immutable sequence/effect DAG. Share unchanged computation, split only when a predicate or state response diverges, and merge worlds when their guarded execution states become equivalent under a declared query.

## Mapping onto Volta
Bitset world support, lane-per-world evaluation, shared immutable nodes, versioned delta queues.

## What this does not establish
Computational counterfactuals are model evaluations, not identified causal effects. Similar numerical states cannot be merged without a valid query-relative equivalence.

## First small executable probe
Two sequence variants share most nodes and diverge at one operator; compare each result to independent execution.

Write a scalar or algebraic fixture and the smallest representative machine path. Prefer one intelligible executable mechanism over an empty API shell. Leave alternative variants visible. Record a failed compile or unresolved semantic issue instead of hiding it or spending the whole campaign on qualification.
