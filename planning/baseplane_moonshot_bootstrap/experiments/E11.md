# E11 — Regional polynomial jets

Numerical owner: `CE-MOON-020` in Cellerator. Sequence consumer: `BP-MOON-040` in Baseplane. Numerical implementation location: `experiments/baseplane_moonshot/families/effects/e11`.

## Question
Can a region cache its local response to a small change in cellular state?

## Concrete mechanism
Store a value, small Jacobian and optional second-order terms around an explicit expansion point. Compose local response jets and route queries inside a trust region through the approximation; outside it, refine/recompute and update the expansion point.

## Mapping onto Volta
FP32/FP64 small matrices, FMA and shared tiles; FP64 is ordinary V100 arithmetic, not FP64 Tensor Cores.

## What this does not establish
Truncated jets are approximate and can become unstable under long composition. A trust score is not a certified error bound unless derived.

## First small executable probe
A known quadratic response with one in-radius and one out-of-radius query; show truncation error honestly.
