# E14 — Learned dynamic chunk boundaries

Owner: `BP-MOON-050`. Suggested implementation location: `experiments/moonshot/families/hierarchy/e14`.

## Question
Can the hierarchy be learned during encoding rather than supplied by annotation?

## Concrete mechanism
Use a small context-dependent boundary scorer, compact boundary carriers and gather an inverse map for dechunking. Train the scorer with a bounded synthetic sequence task and a compute-budget penalty; compare against fixed windows and a cheap surprise-based route.

## Mapping onto Volta
Small half2/FP32 scorer or packed cohort WMMA, ballot/scan boundary compaction, gather/scatter maps.

## What this does not establish
Learned boundaries are model decisions, not discovered biological entities. A label-conditioned segmentation oracle is not allowed in the inference path.

## First small executable probe
An insertion shifts local content: compare fixed versus learned boundary behavior and verify reverse mapping to exact bases.
