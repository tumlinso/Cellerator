## Question
Can a support mask be simultaneously a predicate, a local index and a routing instruction?

## Concrete mechanism
Pair support with rank/select. Intersect two questions in mask space, use rank to address compact descriptors, and select to return to original coordinates. Compose local support maps rather than materializing dense position IDs at each stage.

## What this does not establish
