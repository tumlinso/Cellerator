# E20 — Subscriptions instead of exhaustive features

Owner: `BP-MOON-060`. Suggested implementation location: `experiments/moonshot/families/rendezvous/e20`.

## Question
Can higher-level questions cause the right sequence evidence to be produced?

## Concrete mechanism
Represent a subscription as a typed predicate, region/context restriction and destination ID. Batch equivalent subscriptions, evaluate them against exact sequence or summaries, and route compact answers to the requesting higher-level state. Share repeated predicates across queries.

## Mapping onto Volta
Mask intersections, opcode cohorts, posting lists and warp-aggregated emission.

## What this does not establish
Subscriptions must not leak training labels into inference. A query’s support includes every region actually inspected, not just positive hits.

## First small executable probe
Two requesters share a motif predicate but have different regions and receive different exact source-grounded answers.

Write a scalar or algebraic fixture and the smallest representative machine path. Prefer one intelligible executable mechanism over an empty API shell. Leave alternative variants visible. Record a failed compile or unresolved semantic issue instead of hiding it or spending the whole campaign on qualification.
