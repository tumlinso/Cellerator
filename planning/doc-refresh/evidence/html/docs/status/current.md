## Implemented facilities

The source tree contains a host-first compiler/SDK route and a separately configured CUDA numerical runtime. It also contains typed biological identities, geometry preparation, prepared numerical operations, a bounded native/source relation-update path, and an optional CelleraTorch adapter. The Relation Update Spine v1 record bounds one prepared relation to N1/N16 routes and preserves larger compiler/runtime goals as unfinished. These facilities do not establish complete language coverage, general training, or a complete adaptive execution system.

## Experimental or deliberately bounded

Compiler and runtime coverage remains nonuniform. CSG1 semantic geometry, CPE2 physical images, provider/candidate paths and retained compatibility implementations have different scopes. The CE-LIVE Tensor Core non-promotion concerns its PBMC-derived tile criterion; it does not negate the later CE-GEO exact-cover result. Whole-exchange fusion was not promoted.

## Future intent and outstanding work

The general state-adaptive compilation/gating story and automatic scientific feedback from GlassHelix remain broader intentions. CE-ML2-TRAIN remains in progress in the live Todo authority; this snapshot does not treat its intermediate artifacts as completed general training capability.

## Important qualifications

The host-only compiler/SDK route is not the full accelerator runtime. Test entry points vary by component. Forward, training and adapter envelopes are qualified separately; no repository-wide completion claim establishes them all. Older campaign claims remain bound to their original code and numerical regime.
