# Cellerator evidence selection

Prepared recommendation: see `benchmarks/SHORTLIST.md` and the local result records. At most two studies become highlights. Prefer an explanatory capability/scoped win and a meaningful limit; do not require a positive outcome.

## Implementing editor fills this after reviewing evidence

For every candidate: displayed / supplementary / omitted; reason; original source and any raw export; whether historical or rerun; which unfavorable regimes are retained; claim boundary. This is editorial selection, not a ranking by maximum speedup.

No raw samples or CI bands may be invented from summary medians. Results that cannot be authenticated can be omitted with an explicit limitation rather than a false verification flag.
