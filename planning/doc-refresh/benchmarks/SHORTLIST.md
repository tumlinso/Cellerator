## What the implementing agent fills in

For each selected study: final source/raw paths, actual hardware/software and precision, input/phase definition, command, repeats/order, correctness/quality, evidence hashes, selected/omitted rationale and local review at a source HEAD. The chart/table and captions are already written. A record can be approved as verified historical without pretending to rerun it. Missing raw evidence remains a limitation; it is not backfilled with invented samples.

## Stop rule

No more than two highlighted figures/studies per repo. One bounded refresh per selected study if evidence cannot otherwise be authenticated. One optional new GH capability fixture total. Do not expand source cleanup into a performance investigation when an unchanged historical result already answers the documentation question.
