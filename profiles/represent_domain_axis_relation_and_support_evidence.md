# Structural profile evidence v1

Structural evidence preserves exact relation, structure epoch, domain/axis,
order, geometry, and partition identity slots alongside source and destination
extents. Summary fields cover support counts, degree and occupancy distributions,
strata, co-support, ordering stability, hierarchy evidence, and bounded confidence.

The exact fixture scanner derives support, nonempty destinations, and first two
degree/occupancy moments directly from destination offsets. These statistics are
planning evidence only. They neither replace exact identities nor establish
execution correctness.
